2. What data we collect

unifiedcloudsensors.com does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts of any kind. We only collect personal data that you choose to type into one of the two forms on this site:

We do not request or accept file uploads (e.g. CVs) through these forms at this time. If you attach one to a manual email reply instead, that exchange is handled as ordinary business correspondence, not through this website.

3. Why we process it, and our legal basis

We process the data you submit for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry or application.

  • Contact form — processed under GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (steps taken at your request prior to a possible contract) or, where no contract is in prospect, Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in responding to inbound business inquiries).
  • Careers form — processed under Art. 6(1)(b)/pre-contractual measures, to evaluate you as a candidate, and, where applicable, Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in recruitment).

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent it to any third party.

4. How your submission is handled

When you submit either form, your data is sent to a backend service we operate (AWS Lambda and Amazon API Gateway, hosted in the eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) AWS region) which:

  • sends one notification email containing your submission to our internal inbox, and
  • sends one automatic confirmation email back to the address you provided,

both via Amazon Simple Email Service (SES). Your submission is not written to any database. The backend itself retains no persistent copy — once the two emails are sent, the only copies that exist are the emails themselves, in our company mailbox and in the mailbox you submitted.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. It passes through the following processors, strictly to operate the site and deliver the emails above:

ProcessorRoleLocation

Loading a font this way sends your browser's IP address to Google as a normal part of the request — this happens on every page load, not just when you submit a form, and applies to all visitors, not only those who fill out a form. Google does not receive any of the form data itself.
