## Concept 1 — Weighbridge / truck monitoring — based on brand images 5 & 6

**Status:** start image generated and confirmed on-brand (2026-07-23) — real daylight industrial photo, wifi icon + glowing connection arc to a cloud-and-circuit badge closely matching the real UCS brand icon, no device/text/logo/faces. Video not yet generated (needs Magnific paid tier — free tier only covers image gen).

**Start image:** `images/concept-1-weighbridge-start.png` (generated via ChatGPT)

**Start-image prompt:**
> Photoreal wide shot of an industrial truck stopped on a weighbridge / truck scale, daylight, industrial yard with soft depth of field in the background. A small glowing blue wifi/signal icon hovers near the scale, with a thin glowing blue connection line arcing upward toward a circular cloud-and-circuit badge icon in the upper frame. Color grade in navy `#1D2C49` and electric blue `#476DB8`, soft glow, grounded and photoreal — natural lighting imperfections, not glossy or over-rendered. No tablet or device visible yet — wide establishing shot only. No readable text, no logos, no faces. 16:9 aspect ratio, still image.

**Consolidated video prompt:**
> Wide shot of a truck stopped on a weighbridge, daylight, industrial yard. A thin glowing blue connection line rises from the scale, through a small wifi icon, toward a circular cloud-and-circuit badge icon. The camera slowly pushes in and pans toward a pair of hands (no face visible) holding a tablet that enters the frame, revealing a live dashboard on the tablet screen — a simple line graph and a few colored data cards, matching the glowing blue brand palette. One continuous take, no cuts, real-time pacing, slow deliberate camera move only (push + pan), no handheld shake. No legible text or logos anywhere, no faces. Photoreal, grounded lighting — avoid a glossy, over-rendered CGI look. 16:9 aspect ratio, full-bleed framing, no letterboxing. 8 seconds. No audio/silent.

**Settings:** model Auto · start image only (no end image) · single continuous shot · 8s · no sound · 16:9
