01 — Our Story

Unified Cloud Sensors is an IIoT company based in Ostravačice, building the remote monitoring systems that diagnose the health of industrial weighing equipment before failures cost real money. What started as one patented diagnostic methodology now runs in commercial and pilot installations — technology proven in the field, not on a pitch deck.
